# Free sound effects rollout

## Goal

Introduce a small, replaceable sound-effect layer using CC0 assets before commissioning custom audio.

## Scope

- Reuse the Parent Account sound and celebration settings.
- Keep the sound engine inside the Child route layout.
- Add event-specific sounds for selection, feedback, hints, drag/drop, sorting, locked missions, completion and badges.
- Store source, license and original filenames for every bundled asset.
